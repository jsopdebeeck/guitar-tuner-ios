# Privacy Policy — G Tuner Free

G Tuner Free does not collect, store or share any personal data.

The app uses the microphone only to measure the pitch of the sound in real time. The audio is analysed in memory on your device and is never recorded, saved or transmitted. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and makes no network connections.

Contact: https://github.com/jsopdebeeck/guitar-tuner-ios/issues
